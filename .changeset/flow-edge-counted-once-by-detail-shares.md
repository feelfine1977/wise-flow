By detail shares `abstract` counts a flow edge removed with an element at an
end once, among the paths not shown (`meta.shown.paths`, which the detail
statement reads as "61 of 677 paths"), as by detail shares a flow edge is a
path. It was counted there and again in `meta.abstraction.removedEdges.flow`.

`removedEdges.flow` counts it as before.
