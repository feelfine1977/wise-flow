`labelPlacement: "clear"` on the scene options and on `ProcessMap` moves
paths' labels clear of the cards, the groups' titles, the other labels and
paths, the widest paths first, with a hairline leader where a label had to
move off its line (`placeEdgeLabels`, `EdgeMark.leader`). The default
`midpoint` is unchanged.
