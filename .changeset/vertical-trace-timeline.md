`TraceTimeline` draws a single case down a vertical ruler (`orientation:
"vertical"`): Start and End rings (`terminals`), events of the same minute as
one labelled ring (`clusterMs`), times in a named zone (`timeZone`), stage
colours in their order (`stages`), and elapsed and waiting days in the table.
The contract gains the part `timeline-cluster`.
