A self-loop can be one hairline pill on the card's top-right border
(`OverlayGeometryOptions.selfLoop: "pill"`, `badgeAnchor: "border" | "inside"`;
`OverlayShape.pill`): "↻ 17 %" in the ink, costing the card's name no room,
the badges left of it, scaled with the readable floor like the other pills, in
every renderer. `ProcessMapProps.overlayGeometry` (new) passes the options to
the interactive map. The default arc is unchanged.
